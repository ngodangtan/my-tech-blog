## Why blog in 2025?

Every developer should write. Not because the world needs another blog — there are plenty — but because writing forces clarity. When you can't explain something simply, you don't really understand it.

I've been coding for years, taking notes in scattered files, bookmarking articles I never revisit. This blog is me finally organizing those thoughts into something readable and shareable.

## What I'll write about

- **Swift & iOS** — native mobile development, SwiftUI, Swift concurrency
- **Kotlin & Android** — Jetpack Compose, cross-platform thinking
- **Machine Learning** — on-device ML experiments with Core ML and ONNX
- **Minimalism** — in code and in life

## Who is this for?

Primarily for myself — to solidify my understanding and create a record I can look back on. But if you're a developer interested in mobile, AI, or clean engineering, hopefully something here is useful to you too.

## How often?

No commitments. When I have something worth saying.

Stay tuned.
